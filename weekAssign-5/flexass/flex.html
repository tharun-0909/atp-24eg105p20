Rohit sharma

Rohit Sharma (born April 30, 1987) is an acclaimed Indian international cricketer and current captain, known as the "Hitman" for his aggressive batting. A top-order right-handed batter, he holds records for three ODI double-centuries, most T20I centuries (five), and led India to the 2024 T20 World Cup title. He also led Mumbai Indians to five IPL titles.

Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli (born November 5, 1988) is a legendary Indian cricketer and former national team captain, widely regarded as one of the best batsmen in world cricket. Known as "King Kohli," he holds records for the most ODI centuries (51), fastest to 12,000+ ODI runs, and the highest IPL scorer, having retired from Test and T20I cricket in 2025 and 2024 respectively.

Dhoni

Mahendra Singh Dhoni (born July 7, 1981) is a legendary Indian cricketer and former captain, renowned as "Captain Cool" for his calm leadership and for winning all three major ICC trophies: the 2007 T20 World Cup, 2011 ODI World Cup, and 2013 Champions Trophy. A prolific wicketkeeper-batsman, he led Chennai Super Kings (CSK) to five IPL titles.
